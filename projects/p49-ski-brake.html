P49 Ski Brake

This ski brake was specifically designed for the Dynafit P49 Binding to comply with the new ISMF race brake regulations. While the P49 Binding was originally crafted as the pinnacle of lightweight race performance, these updated regulations threatened to make it obsolete.

This innovative brake design seamlessly attaches to the P49, extending its relevance for elite skimo racers seeking top-tier race performance. At just 50 grams per brake, for a total binding weight of 99 grams, this system remains the lightest binding/brake combination that meets the updated ISMF racing standards.

  • Brake 50 g
  • Binding + brake 99 g
  • Binding Dynafit P49
  • Standard ISMF race brake rules
