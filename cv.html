Research Experience

University of Colorado Boulder

August 2023 – Present
PhD Candidate

Investigated how soft materials, compliant structures, and surface textures influence flapping-fin propulsion efficiency and control of bioinspired uncrewed underwater vehicles (UUVs).

  • Designed and built a 4-axis gantry system for repeatable evaluation of robotic fin propulsion in a 2-meter-long tank.
  • Developed shark-inspired sub-mm surface textures using SLA printing to reduce drag and delay flow separation.
  • Prototyped and tested silicone fins to quantify efficiency and thrust tradeoffs between soft and rigid propulsors.
  • Led a senior design team to build a fin-propelled robot for testing fin propulsion and autonomous swimming control.
  • Built a low cost (<$500), penguin-inspired UUV capable of agile maneuvers using only two control surfaces.
